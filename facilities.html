Our Spaces

Three distinct settings for one unforgettable day

The Great Barn

👥 200 guests 👁 4,500 sq ft

A restored 1890s barn with exposed timber beams, a polished concrete floor, and wall-to-wall windows facing the gardens. Heated in winter, cooled by ceiling fans year-round.

The Garden Terrace

👥 300 guests 🌱 2 acres

Open-air ceremony surrounded by lavender, climbing roses, and a 200-year-old oak. A floating white aisle leads to a stone arch draped in seasonal blooms.

Lakeside Pergola

👥 150 guests 🗈 1,200 sq ft

A timber pergola overlooking a private lake, draped in cascading bougainvillea. Perfect for cocktail hour, photo sessions, or an intimate rehearsal dinner.

Common Questions

Everything you want to know before booking

What dates are available?

We host a maximum of 2 weddings per month (spring and autumn) to ensure dedicated service. Dates fill 8–12 months in advance.

Can we bring our own vendors?

Absolutely. We have a preferred vendor list, but no required vendors. You're free to work with anyone.

Is the venue rain-proof?

The Great Barn is heated and cooled. The outdoor spaces can be covered with clear-span tents. Many couples do a garden ceremony followed by a barn reception.

What's the rental timeline?

Access begins 3 days before your event for setup. Your event day allows from 10:00 AM until 1:00 AM the following morning. Breakdown begins at 7:00 AM Sunday.

Is noise restricted?

Licensed music can continue until midnight on weekdays and 1:00 AM on weekends. Sound consultants are available upon request.
